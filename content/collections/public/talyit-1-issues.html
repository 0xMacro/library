Interoperability addressed fb4ccc96927e66f06e25fb158572cc451aab8b5e ## [I-2] Market views have lifecycle, pricing, and accounting caveats `Market`'s views return their intended values, but their meanings are narrower than an integration might infer from their names: - **Trading availability:** `buysPaused()` reports only the timed pause. It can return `false` while `ANNOUNCED` or `ARCHIVED` lifecycle gates prohibit buying. - **Frozen state:** After archive, `supplyOf()`, `liability()`, and `mid()` retain their values at time of archive, while claims reduce holder balances and reserves. They do not describe remaining unclaimed shares or payment obligations. - **Pricing and units:** `mid()` is a floored marginal price, not an executable quote (the actual trade price depends on trade size and spread). `mid()` also uses 18 decimals, whereas `settlementRate()` uses six; they should not be interpreted at the same scale. - **Zero settlement rates:** `settlementRate()` can be zero both before and after archive for an empty or dust-funded side. It is not a substitute for `lifecycle()`. Misinterpreting these values could cause incorrect displays or quotes, reverting transactions, or false insolvency alerts in consuming applications. These are not contract defects, but informational integration caveats. **Fix-review disposition (I-2): Addressed through documentation**, commit `fb4ccc9`. Market NatSpec and README integration caveats clarify these meanings; all five new view-semantics tests pass. No view implementation was changed.
