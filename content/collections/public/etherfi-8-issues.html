Protocol Design low medium fixed f2cd3a59bc71e013f8409365a49ecbf7b1eae37e ## [L-1] Pause cannot halt a single venue In `SafeBorrower.sol`, `pause()` sets the single paused flag checked by `whenNotPaused` on all major actions. These changes allow vaults to interact with multiple venues, where one deployment serves every configured Aave pool, Morpho instance and/or potential forks, so the pauser can only halt all of them at once. On an incident specific to one venue, the pauser must halt borrowing and collateral withdrawal on every healthy venue, and wait for the owner to configure the market, which is expected to be delayed due to a timelock. **Remediations to Consider** Consider allowing venues to be paused individually as well as globally to allow for handling of venue specific incidents without effecting other healthy venues.
